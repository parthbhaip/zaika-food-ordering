About Zaika

Kolhapur's kitchens, one tap away

Zaika started with a simple idea: the city's best misal, tambda rassa and late-night biryani shouldn't need a free evening and a parking spot. We bring them to your door, hot.

How Zaika works

  1. Pick a kitchen

    Browse restaurants near you by cuisine, rating or delivery time. Every menu shows real prices, veg marks and what's sold out today.

  2. The restaurant cooks

    Your order goes straight to the kitchen. You'll see the moment it's accepted, while it's being prepared, and when it's packed.

  3. A rider brings it over

    A local delivery partner picks it up and heads your way. Share the 4-digit code at the door and it's yours.

What we care about

Honest bills

Item prices come from the restaurant. Delivery fee, platform fee and GST are listed line by line before you pay, never added later.

Local first

Most of our partners are family-run kitchens from Rajarampuri to Rankala. A good review from you helps them more than any ad could.

Fair to riders

Delivery partners choose when they go online and see what each delivery pays before they take it.
