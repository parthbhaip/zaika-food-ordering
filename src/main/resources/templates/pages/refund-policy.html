Cancellations and refunds

Last updated 1 October 2026

Food is cooked fresh for every order, so once a kitchen starts on yours we can't take it back. Here's exactly when you can cancel and how refunds work.

Cancelling an order yourself

  • You can cancel from the order's tracking page while it says Placed, before the restaurant accepts it. Tell us why in a word or two; it helps the restaurant.
  • Once the restaurant accepts, the cancel button disappears because cooking has started. If you really need to stop an order, call us and we'll do what we can with the restaurant.

When the restaurant or Zaika cancels

Sometimes a restaurant has to decline an order (an item has run out, or the kitchen is closing), or we cancel one because no rider is available. In those cases you always get a full refund, including delivery and platform fees.

Online refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank. For cash on delivery orders, item refunds are sent to a UPI ID or bank account you share with our support team.

Problems with a delivered order

If something is missing, wrong or damaged, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order code (like ZK-26-000123) and, if you can, a photo. We'll check with the restaurant and reply within one working day.
