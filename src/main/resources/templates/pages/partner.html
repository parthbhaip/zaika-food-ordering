Become a delivery partner

Why restaurants choose Zaika

  • Orders on one screen

    New orders appear on your live board. Accept with a prep time, mark it ready, and the rider is already on the way.

  • Your menu, your prices

    Add dishes with photos, mark bestsellers, and switch an item off the moment it sells out. Changes show instantly.

  • Clear daily numbers

    Today's orders, revenue and average order value on your dashboard, with reports you can download any time.

Why riders ride with us

  • Your hours

    Tap online when you're free, offline when you're done. No shifts to book and no minimum hours.

  • Pay you can see

    Every delivery shows its payout before you claim it: a base amount plus a share of the delivery fee.

  • Short city runs

    Pick-ups and drops stay inside Kolhapur, so you spend your time riding, not crossing the district.

How joining works

  1. Sign up online

    Create your partner account and tell us about your kitchen or your vehicle. No paperwork at this stage.

  2. We review it

    Our partner team checks your details, usually within 2 working days. Your dashboard shows the status all along.

  3. Start taking orders

    Once approved, your restaurant goes live for customers, or you can go online and claim your first delivery.

Questions before you sign up?
